# 0.3 以来新增内容的历史悬浮指南

本轮新增 8 条历史指南，在每种语言中选取 18 处正文与商品说明设置 19 个链接，其中 4 个复用原有指南。只补真实历史背景，不改事件叙事和奖惩。核对日期：2026-10-15。

## 条目与出处

### 湖广填四川

元末明初已有湖广籍人口迁入川渝，明末清初以后又形成大规模移民潮。来人除今湖北、湖南一带，也有江西、广东、福建等地居民，迁徙改变了当地的聚落、宗族与生活习俗。

English: Migrants from Huguang entered Sichuan and Chongqing during the late Yuan and early Ming. Further large movements followed the Ming–Qing transition. Settlers also came from Jiangxi, Guangdong, Fujian and other regions, reshaping settlements, kinship networks and local customs.

出处：[重庆社会科学，黄梅，2017年第3期：“湖广填四川”移民现象的历史考察](https://cqsk.cbpt.cnki.net/portal/journal/portal/client/paper/faeba5ad927ba34f8e9cce3ff407ec7c)；[重庆市农业农村委员会：荣昌：路孔古镇 寻迹“烟雨古寨、移民水乡”的文化记忆](https://nyncw.cq.gov.cn/zwxx_161/qxlb/202307/t20230711_12139451.html)。

### 同乡会馆

外来商人和移民按乡籍集资设馆，联络同乡、祭祀聚会，也维护共同的商贸利益。清代重庆留下多处会馆，禹王宫、齐安公所与广东公所构成今天重庆湖广会馆的主体。

English: Migrant communities and merchants pooled funds for halls organized by place of origin. They maintained hometown ties, hosted communal worship and gatherings, and defended shared commercial interests. Chongqing’s surviving Huguang complex includes Yuwang Palace, the Qi’an Hall and the Guangdong Hall.

出处：[重庆市档案馆、渝中区档案馆：跟着档案游巴蜀：雕梁载史 馆韵长流——重庆湖广会馆](https://jda.cq.gov.cn/web/article/web/content_1461428557456216064.html)；[重庆市农业农村委员会：荣昌：路孔古镇 寻迹“烟雨古寨、移民水乡”的文化记忆](https://nyncw.cq.gov.cn/zwxx_161/qxlb/202307/t20230711_12139451.html)。

### 蜀绣

蜀绣以成都为主要传承地，绣工用丝线在底料上逐针绣出纹样。蜀锦则由织工在织机上组织经纬成花。两种技艺都与蜀地丝业相连，制成花纹的方法不同。

English: Centred on Chengdu, Shu embroidery creates patterns by stitching silk thread onto a ground fabric. Shu brocade forms its patterns through the arrangement of warp and weft on a loom. Both belong to Sichuan’s silk traditions and require distinct skills.

出处：[中国非物质文化遗产网，四川省非物质文化遗产保护中心：蜀绣最高境界：异色 异形 双面](https://www.ihchina.cn/news_1_details/9709.html)；[中国非物质文化遗产网：蜀锦：锦绣丝织，巧夺天工](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/21921.html)。

### 蜀地竹纸

四川夹江等地传承以嫩竹造纸的技艺。纸工浸泡、蒸煮竹料，反复洗料捣浆，再用竹帘抄成纸页、压水干燥。一张竹纸的成色，既取决于原料，也取决于多道工序中的手艺。

English: Jiajiang and other Sichuan communities preserve bamboo papermaking traditions. Makers soak and cook young bamboo, wash and pound it into pulp, form sheets with bamboo screens, then press and dry them. Paper quality depends on both the material and skilled work throughout the process.

出处：[夹江县人民政府：国家级非物质文化遗产——竹纸制作技艺工艺简介](https://www.jiajiang.gov.cn/jjx/fwzwhyc/631a7a181e30468398596a5e3.html)。

### 大熊猫

四川宝兴邓池沟是大熊猫模式标本的产地。1869年，博物学家阿尔芒·戴维在当地取得标本，送往法国研究，大熊猫由此进入近代动物学的记录与分类。

English: Dengchigou in Baoxing, Sichuan, is the type locality of the giant panda. In 1869, naturalist Armand David obtained specimens there and sent them to France for study, bringing the animal into modern zoological description and classification.

出处：[国家林业和草原局：认识大熊猫（七）](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/zhzs/511354.jhtml)；[国家林业和草原局，中国大熊猫保护研究中心：大熊猫的历史](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/dzw/579507.jhtml)。

### 自贡恐龙化石

自贡一带保存着丰富的侏罗纪恐龙化石。当地的近代调查始于二十世纪；1979年末，大山铺化石群大规模暴露，此后的发掘为研究恐龙骨骼、古环境与地层提供了重要材料。

English: Zigong preserves rich Jurassic dinosaur deposits. Modern investigations began in the twentieth century. Large exposures at Dashanpu in late 1979 led to excavations that supplied important evidence for dinosaur anatomy, ancient environments and geological strata.

出处：[自贡日报，曾上游、陈蓓艳：恐龙在自贡的“复活”史](https://dp.zgm.cn/show/43222)；[四川省地方志工作办公室：四川省地方志工作办公室：四川历史文化图文资料](https://www.scsqw.cn/upload/main/contentmanage/article/file/201809071553253757.pdf)。

### 日影、漏刻与测候

日晷利用日影测时，漏刻依滴水与水位变化计刻，两者可互相参校。观测星辰另有专门仪器：元代郭守敬改进浑仪结构、创制简仪，用于测量天体位置。

English: Sundials measure time from a shadow; water clocks use flowing water and changes in water level. Observers could compare these readings. Astronomical instruments served another purpose: Yuan scholar Guo Shoujing simplified the armillary sphere to create the jianyi for measuring celestial positions.

出处：[故宫博物院：日晷](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/240224.html)；[故宫博物院：兽耳八卦铜壶滴漏](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/clock/234695.html)；[中国国家博物馆：简仪模型](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/kgfjp/202111/t20211116_252346.shtml)。

### 大足石刻

大足石刻的重要造像多成于九至十三世纪，题材兼有佛教、道教、儒家思想与日常生活。石壁题记还保存了年代、人物与信仰的信息，使造像之外的地方社会也有迹可寻。

English: Major groups of the Dazu carvings date from the ninth to thirteenth centuries. They combine Buddhist, Daoist and Confucian subjects with scenes of daily life. Inscriptions preserve dates, names and religious information, offering evidence about the communities around the sculptures.

出处：[联合国教科文组织世界遗产中心：Dazu Rock Carvings](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/912/)。
